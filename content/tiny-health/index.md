## Select Projects

I put together a list of three projects that I hope highlight the scope of my work. The first two are peer-reviewed publications and the third is from an explanatory science article.

1. **Hypocolypse**: A longitudinal study looking at the periods before, during, and after an acute hypoxic event over a Caribbean coral reef. I used both 16S rRNA and shotgun metagenomic analysis to track changes in microbial communities. 
2. **ProjectDIGEST**: The gut microbiomes of dominant herbivorous fish species on coral reefs. I used 16S rRNA analysis to demonstrate  that microbiome composition was highly specific to host species.
3. **Picture the unseen**: Analyses I performed for a [Smithsonian News Piece](https://stri.si.edu/story/picture-unseen) I wrote with a colleague about how we use [anvi'o](https://anvio.org/) to understand microbial diversity. I used shotgun metagenomic analysis on publically available data from the TARA Oceans dataset.
