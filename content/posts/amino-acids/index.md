## Amino Acid Properties

| Amino Acid | 3-letter / 1-letter | Unique Feature / Special Property | Biological Significance |
|:-----| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Alanine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alanine) | Ala / A | Small, chemically **inert** methyl side-chain. | Used as the baseline in mutation studies (**Alanine scanning**). |
| [Arginine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arginine) | Arg / R | Positively charged; contains a highly basic **guanidinium group**. | Forms complex **salt bridges** and hydrogen bond networks. |
| [Asparagine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asparagine) ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀ ⠀  | Asn / N | Polar carboxamide group; prone to **deamidation**. | Primary site for **N-linked glycosylation**. |
| [Aspartic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspartate) | Asp / D | Negatively charged carboxylate group; **short** side-chain. | Coordinates **metal ions** (like Mg2+) in enzyme active sites. |
| [Cysteine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cysteine) | Cys / C | Contains a highly reactive **thiol (-SH) group**. | Forms **disulfide bonds** to stabilize tertiary/quaternary structures. |
| [Glutamic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glutamic_acid) | Glu / E | Negatively charged carboxylate group; **longer** side-chain. | Acts as the primary **excitatory neurotransmitter** in the brain. |
| [Glutamine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glutamine) | Gln / Q | Polar carboxamide group; highly flexible. | Acts as the primary **nitrogen donor** in metabolic pathways. |
| [Glycine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glycine) | Gly / G | **Smallest** amino acid; hydrogen side-chain; no chiral center. | Provides high conformational **flexibility** in protein backbones. |
| [Histidine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Histidine) | His / H | **Imidazole ring** with a pKa near physiological pH (~6.0). | Actively switches charges; vital **proton donor/acceptor** in enzymes. |
| [Isoleucine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoleucine) | Ile / I | Branched-chain with **two chiral centers**. | Packages tightly within **hydrophobic interiors**. |
| [Leucine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leucine) | Leu / L | Branched-chain; most **abundant** amino acid in proteins. | Crucial for **structural stability** and signaling pathways. |
| [Lysine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lysine) | Lys / K | Positively charged; long, flexible aliphatic chain with **primary amine**. | Frequent target for **ubiquitination** and acetylation. |
| [Methionine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methionine) | Met / M | Thioether side-chain; contains **sulfur**. | Serves as the universal **start codon** (AUG) for translation. |
| [Phenylalanine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenylalanine) | Phe / F | Highly hydrophobic **benzene ring** side-chain. | Absorbs UV light; crucial for **aromatic stacking**. |
| [Proline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proline) | Pro / P | **Cyclic** imino acid; side-chain bonds to nitrogen backbone. | Creates **kinks or breaks** in alpha-helices; rigid structure. |
| [Serine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serine) | Ser / S | Polar uncharged; contains a highly **reactive hydroxyl group**. | Core component of **catalytic triads** (e.g., serine proteases). |
| [Threonine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Threonine) | Thr / T | Polar uncharged; contains **two chiral centers**. | Frequent target for **O-linked glycosylation**. |
| [Tryptophan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tryptophan) | Trp / W | **Largest** amino acid; contains a bulky **indole ring**. | Strongest **UV absorber** at 280nm; precursor to serotonin. |
| [Tyrosine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrosine) | Tyr / Y | Amphipathic; contains a **reactive hydroxyl group** on a phenyl ring. | Major site for **phosphorylation** in cellular signaling. |
| [Valine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valine) | Val / V | Branched-chain hydrocarbon; highly **hydrophobic**. | Stabilizes protein cores via **hydrophobic interactions**. |
{caption="Amino acid properties and their significance in biological systems"}




## Amino Acid Structure

| Amino Acid    |  Abbrev. |  Code  | MW (Da) | Catagory   | R-group                      | Codon notation |
|---------------|:--------:|:------:|:-------:|:----------:|------------------------------|----------------|
| Alanine       |   Ala    |    A   |    89   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH3}$                   | GCN |
| Arginine      |   Arg    |    R   |   174   |    basic   | $\ce{(CH2)3NH-C(NH)NH2}$     | CGN, AGR[^1]|
| Asparagine    |   Asn    |    N   |   132   |    polar   | $\ce{CH2C(O)NH2}$            | AAY |
| Aspartic acid |   Asp    |    D   |   133   |   acidic   | $\ce{CH2C(O)OH}$             | GAY |
| Cysteine      |   Cys    |    C   |   121   |    polar   | $\ce{CH2SH}$                 | UGY |
| Glutamic acid |   Glu    |    E   |   147   |   acidic   | $\ce{CH2CH2C(O)OH}$          | GAR |
| Glutamine     |   Gln    |    Q   |   146   |    polar   | $\ce{CH2CH2C(O)NH2}$         | CAR |
| Glycine       |   Gly    |    G   |    75   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{H}$                     | GGN |
| Histidine     |   His    |    H   |   155   |    basic   | $\ce{CH2-C3H3N2}$            | CAY |
| Isoleucine    |   Ile    |    I   |   131   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH(CH3)CH2CH3}$         | AUH |
| Leucine       |   Leu    |    L   |   131   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH2CH(CH3)2}$           | CUN, UUR[^2]|
| Lysine        |   Lys    |    K   |   146   |    basic   | $\ce{(CH2)4NH2}$             | AAR |
| Methionine    |   Met    |    M   |   149   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH2CH2SCH3}$            | AUG |
| Phenylalanine |   Phe    |    F   |   165   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH2C6H5}$               | UUY |
| Proline       |   Pro    |    P   |   115   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH2CH2CH2-}$            | CCN |
| Serine        |   Ser    |    S   |   105   |    polar   | $\ce{CH2OH}$                 | UCN, AGY |
| Threonine     |   Thr    |    T   |   119   |    polar   | $\ce{CH(OH)CH3}$             | ACN |
| Tryptophan    |   Trp    |    W   |   204   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH2C8H6N}$              | UGG |
| Tyrosine      |   Tyr    |    Y   |   181   |    polar   | $\ce{CH2-C6H4OH}$            | UAY |
| Valine        |   Val    |    V   |   117   |  nonpolar  | $\ce{CH(CH3)2}$              | GUN |
{caption="Amino acid classification by R group properties"}

[^1]: Can also be coded by MGR, CGY  
[^2]: Can also be coded by YUR, CUY
